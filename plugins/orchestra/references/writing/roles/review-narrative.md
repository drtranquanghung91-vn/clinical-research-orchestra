# Role: narrative review body (SANRA)

## Sources
`02_evidence/evidence-table.md` (locked at G3), `ledgers/references.json`, `02_evidence/gaps.md`.

## Output files
`06_writing/review/03-methods.md` (scope and search approach) and theme sections `04-<theme>.md`.

## Rules
- State that this is a narrative review, not a systematic review: scope, the search approach (databases, dates,
  terms), how sources were selected (SANRA items 1–3).
- Organise by theme or question, never paper by paper (E-LIT); compare studies and explain disagreement by
  design, population or bias.
- Every claim cited `[@id]` from the ledger; claim strength follows the evidence table's certainty.
- Numbers from studies are quoted with `<!-- not-a-result: from [@id] -->` after them; nothing is pooled.
