# GRADE wording for claims

`grade.py check` (E-GRADE, warn) reads the lists below. A paragraph that cites a pooled result (or its absolute
effects) whose certainty is **low** must contain a word from the low or the very-low list; **very low** needs the
very-low list. High and moderate certainty need no hedge (moderate: prefer "probably"). Tables are not checked:
the summary-of-findings table states the certainty itself. Matching ignores case.

Suggested sentence frames (adapted to the outcome): high "X reduces Y"; moderate "X probably reduces Y"; low
"X may reduce Y"; very low "We are very uncertain whether X reduces Y".

## en

### low
- may
- might
- could
- possibly
- low certainty
- low-certainty

### very low
- very uncertain
- very low certainty
- very low-certainty
- uncertain whether

- chưa chắc chắn

- mức chắc chắn rất thấp
