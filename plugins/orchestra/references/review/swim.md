# Structured narrative synthesis (SWiM)

When the protocol says pooling is not appropriate (clinical or methodological diversity, incompatible measures,
fewer than 2 studies), write a structured narrative synthesis following the SWiM items (paraphrased):

1. Grouping: how studies were grouped for synthesis and why.
2. Standardised metric: the effect metric used for each group and any transformation.
3. Method: the synthesis method (for example summary statistics of effects, combining p-values, vote counting
   based on direction of effect — never counting "significant" results) and why.
4. Priority: criteria used to prioritise results (for example lower risk of bias, larger studies).
5. Heterogeneity: how variation in effects was explored.
6. Certainty: how certainty was assessed (GRADE).
7. Presentation: tables and plots used (an effect-direction plot or a forest plot without a pooled diamond).
8. Results: what the synthesis shows, with each study's estimate from the results ledger.
9. Limitations of the synthesis.

The SWiM guideline citation is looked up with the `literature-search` skill when the methods cite it; never typed.
