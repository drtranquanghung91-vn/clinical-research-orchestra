# PROSPERO record (draft)

`03_protocol/prospero.md` is a draft the author pastes into PROSPERO; nothing is submitted by a tool. Its first
line says "Status: not registered" until the author pastes the registration number into
`project.yaml → review.registration`; then the line becomes "Registered: <number>". Scoping reviews are not
accepted by PROSPERO: register them elsewhere (for example OSF) or state "not registered".

Fields to draft, each from the locked protocol (copy, never invent): review title; anticipated or actual start
and completion dates [[AUTHOR: …]]; stage of the review at submission; named contact and review team
[[AUTHOR: …]]; funding and conflicts of interest [[AUTHOR: …]]; review question; searches (databases, dates,
restrictions); link to the search strategy; condition or domain; participants; interventions or exposures;
comparators; types of study; context; main outcomes (with timing and effect measure); additional outcomes; data
extraction (two extractors, consensus); risk of bias (tool per design); strategy for data synthesis (model,
heterogeneity, when not to pool); subgroup analyses (the one factor in the register); type and method of review;
language; country; dissemination plans; keywords.
