## Correction recorded by the source author
QUADAS-3 was first cited by its 2025 piloting paper, with the domains described as in QUADAS-2. Cite
the final tool (Whiting et al., Ann Intern Med, February 2026) and its four domains: Participants,
Index Test, Target Condition, Analysis.
