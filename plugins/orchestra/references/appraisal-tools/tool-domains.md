# Key domains by tool (summary, paraphrased)

Methods content by Amelia Le (co-author), adapted for clinical-research-orchestra. Source: `critical-appraisal-vi` v3.

Orientation only. The official forms are authoritative (see `tools-by-design.md`).

**RoB 2 (RCT)**: 5 domains.
1. Randomisation process: random sequence, allocation concealment, baseline imbalance that hints at a problem.
2. Deviations from intended interventions: who knew the allocation; deviations arising from the trial context; an analysis that fits (ITT for the effect of assignment).
3. Missing outcome data: proportion, balance between arms, reasons related to the outcome, sensitivity analysis.
4. Outcome measurement: appropriate method, same in both arms, whether assessors knew the allocation, subjective outcomes.
5. Selection of the reported result: follows a pre-specified plan (protocol, SAP, registry); not picked from several measurements or analyses.

**ROBINS-I V2 (non-randomised interventions)**: first define the target trial and the important confounders. Domains: confounding; classification of the intervention (including immortal time); selection of participants into the analysis; deviations from intended interventions; missing data; outcome measurement; selection of the reported result.

**ROBINS-E (exposures)**: confounding; exposure measurement; selection of participants; post-exposure interventions or events; missing data; outcome measurement; selection of the reported result.

**NOS**: cohort: selection (representativeness of the exposed group, non-exposed from the same source, ascertainment of exposure, outcome not present at start), comparability (control of the main confounders), outcome (assessment, enough follow-up, loss to follow-up). Case-control: case definition, representativeness of cases, selection of controls from the community or the same source, control definition; comparability; exposure (ascertainment, same method for cases and controls, non-response rate).

**AXIS (cross-sectional)**: aims and design fit; sample size justified; target population and sampling frame; representative selection; non-responders handled and described; valid, tested measurement of exposure and outcome; statistical methods; results consistent with methods; conclusions supported by the data; limitations; funding and conflicts of interest; ethical approval.

**JBI prevalence**: suitable sampling frame, way of sampling, adequate sample size, description of subjects and setting, coverage of the analysis, valid and standardised way of identifying the condition, appropriate statistics, response rate and its handling.

**CASP Qualitative**: clear aims; qualitative method appropriate; design suits the aims; recruitment strategy; data collection that answers the question; researcher–participant relationship (reflexivity); ethical issues; sufficiently rigorous analysis; clear findings; value of the research.

**MMAT**: 2 screening questions plus 5 criteria for each type (qualitative, RCT, quantitative non-randomised, quantitative descriptive, mixed methods: reason for combining, integration, interpretation of divergences).

**QUADAS-3 (diagnostic accuracy)**: 6 phases: state the review question; define the "ideal diagnostic accuracy test"; draw a flow diagram for each estimate; rate risk of bias in 4 domains (Participants, Index Test, Target Condition, Analysis; Target Condition replaces the QUADAS-2 domain "Flow and Timing"); rate applicability; overall judgement. Rate each **accuracy estimate**, not the whole study.

**PROBAST+AI**: model development (16 questions) and model evaluation (18 questions); domains: participants and data sources, predictors, outcome, analysis; applicability for the first three domains.

**AMSTAR 2 (systematic reviews)**: 16 items, 7 critical: protocol registered before the review; comprehensive search; justification for excluded studies; risk of bias assessed in included studies; appropriate meta-analytic methods; risk of bias considered when interpreting results; publication bias assessed. Overall confidence: High / Moderate / Low / Critically low by the number of critical flaws (no summed score).
