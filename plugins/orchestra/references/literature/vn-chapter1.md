# Vietnamese thesis Chapter 1 (Tổng quan tài liệu)

## Typical outline
- **1.1 Khái niệm và định nghĩa**: definitions and classification with the current guideline criteria
  (name the guideline and year; check for renamed conditions).
- **1.2 Dịch tễ học**: worldwide, then in Vietnam.
- **1.3 Cơ chế bệnh sinh**: only as far as the research question needs.
- **1.4 Chẩn đoán và điều trị**: only what bears on the study variables.
- **1.5 Các nghiên cứu trên thế giới**.
- **1.6 Các nghiên cứu tại Việt Nam**.
- **1.7 Khoảng trống và cách nghiên cứu này giải quyết**: each gap from `02_evidence/gaps.md`, with its
  counter-check, tied to an objective.

Sections 1.5 and 1.6 are still synthesised by theme within each section (see `review-writing.md`); a
list of studies in date order is not acceptable. Compare Vietnamese and foreign findings explicitly and
explain differences (population, setting, definitions).

## Numbering
Headings 1.1, 1.1.1; tables `Bảng 1.1`; figures `Hình 1.1`; captions above tables and below figures
(follow the institution's template if it differs). Number references with `cite_render.py`, never by hand.

## Length
Follow the training institution's regulation on chapter length. If none is given, ask the author and
record the decision in `decisions.md`.

## Citation style
Vietnamese and English references both follow `project.yaml → citation_style`; Vietnamese authors keep
the family name first as published, and a Vietnamese-language source keeps its original title.
