# Committee question bank

Use as a menu, not a script. Choose only the questions this study invites. Adapt wording to the project language.

## Vietnamese committee conventions

- Thesis defence (bảo vệ luận văn/luận án): the candidate presents 15-20 minutes. The reviewer (phản biện) reads the
  thesis first and asks the sharpest questions; then other members, then the chair. Expect questions on whether the
  objectives (mục tiêu) match the conclusions (kết luận) and recommendations (kiến nghị), and on what the candidate did
  personally.
- Typical asks: "Why this design and not another?", "Why this sample size?", "What is new (tính mới) and what is the
  practical value (ý nghĩa thực tiễn)?", "What are the limitations (hạn chế)?", "What would you change?"
- Examiners often ask for a definition, cut-off or scale and its source (tiêu chuẩn chẩn đoán, ngưỡng, thang điểm). Have
  the guideline and its year ready.
- Poster session: 2-3 minutes at the board, with interruptions. Prepare a 30-second summary and one-sentence answers.
- Ethics and data: ethics approval number (số quyết định của hội đồng đạo đức), consent, who owns the data.
- Answer shape: answer first, evidence second, limitation third. Never bluff: "this was not analysed; it is a limitation".

## Questions by section

### Methods choice
- Why this design (cross-sectional, case-control, cohort, trial) for this question?
- Why this population, site and period? Who was excluded and why?
- How were exposure and outcome defined and measured? Was the measure validated?
- Retrospective data: how complete and reliable were the records?

### Sample size
- How was n calculated? Which effect size, alpha, power and prior study?
- Was the target reached? If not, what does that do to precision?
- Consecutive or convenience sampling? What selection bias follows?

### Confounding and bias
- Which confounders did you consider? Why these in the model, and how many events per variable?
- Confounding by indication, selection bias, information bias, recall bias: which apply?
- Is the association causal? Which Bradford Hill points are met?

### Missing data
- How much was missing, per variable? Complete-case or imputation, and why?
- Could missingness depend on the outcome?

### Multiplicity and post-hoc
- How many tests were run? Was any correction applied?
- Which analyses were pre-specified in the SAP and which were added later? Are the latter labelled exploratory?
- Any subgroup claim without an interaction test?

### Generalisability
- To whom do the results apply: one hospital, one province, one period?
- How does the sample differ from the national or published population?

### Clinical meaning
- Is the effect large enough to matter to a patient (MCID)? Statistical significance is not clinical relevance.
- Wide confidence interval: which range of effects is still compatible with the data?
- What would you tell a clinician to do on Monday? What would you not conclude?
- What does this add to the literature (see the evidence table) and what contradicts it?

### Poster and abstract extras
- What is the one message? Which single number matters most, and where is its CI?
- Do the abstract, poster and thesis give identical numbers?
