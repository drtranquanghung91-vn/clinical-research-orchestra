### Important follow-up test results

Liver enzymes and eosinophil count normalised by outpatient follow-up, and renal function returned
close to its pre-illness baseline (see the values above). A later follow-up visit, after the
corticosteroid taper was completed, confirmed no relapse of rash and normal thyroid function.

### Adherence and tolerability

Adherence and tolerability were assessed by clinical interview at the day +43 and day +62
outpatient follow-up visits. The oral prednisolone taper was completed as prescribed, with no
missed doses and no reported difficulty tolerating the corticosteroid course.

### Adverse and unanticipated events

No adverse events beyond the index drug reaction and its direct complications (transient liver and
kidney impairment) were reported during follow-up. Allopurinol was not reintroduced, and the
culprit drug was recorded in the patient's allergy record; his gout has since been managed with
lifestyle measures and colchicine for flares, with rheumatology to consider an alternative
urate-lowering drug after allergy consultation.
