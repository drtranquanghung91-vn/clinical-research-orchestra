## Therapeutic intervention

### Type of intervention

Pharmacological: withdrawal of the suspected culprit and co-prescribed drugs, systemic
corticosteroids, and supportive care.

### Dosage, strength and duration

Allopurinol (already self-discontinued by the patient) and colchicine were stopped. Intravenous
methylprednisolone was given at {{R-A0-016}} mg/day (equivalent to {{R-A0-017}} mg/kg/day for this
patient's body weight of {{R-A0-018}} kg) from day 0 to day +6, followed by oral prednisolone
{{R-A0-028}} mg/day, tapered over {{R-A0-029}} weeks. Antihistamines, a topical corticosteroid
cream and intravenous fluid support were also given. Nephrology was consulted; dialysis was not
required.

### Changes to the intervention

No dose escalation or change in the choice of immunosuppressant was needed; the oral prednisolone
dose was reduced progressively, as tolerated, over the taper period described above, guided by
resolution of the rash and by liver and kidney function.
