## Patient information

A man in his 50s presented with fever and a widespread rash. He had gout, with flares of the first
metatarsophalangeal joint several times a year, self-treated with colchicine during flares; and
hypertension treated with amlodipine 5 mg/day, which had been stable. He had chronic kidney disease,
with a pre-illness baseline creatinine of {{R-A0-042}} µmol/L and an estimated glomerular filtration
rate of {{R-A0-043}} mL/min/1.73<!-- not-a-result: standard eGFR unit denominator, not a measured value --> m². He had no known drug allergy before this episode, did not smoke
and drank alcohol lightly, and continued his usual work and daily activities before this episode.
Family history was not recorded. His HLA-B*58:01 genotype was unknown at that time, since this test
had not been performed before starting the drug that is discussed below.

His main concern at the outpatient visit before this episode was a painful gout flare. Serum uric
acid was {{R-A0-001}} µmol/L, and allopurinol {{R-A0-002}} mg/day was started for long-term urate
lowering, together with prophylactic colchicine; the allopurinol dose was not reduced for his known
chronic kidney disease. No HLA-B*58:01 testing was performed before starting the drug.
